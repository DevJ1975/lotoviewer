# Periodic inspection of energy control procedures (29 CFR 1910.147(c)(6))

## How often

The employer must inspect each energy control procedure **at least once a year**. The inspection makes sure the procedure and the standard's requirements are being followed (1910.147(c)(6)(i)).

## Who inspects

The inspection is done by an **authorized employee other than the one(s) using the procedure** being inspected (1910.147(c)(6)(i)(A)). It is meant to catch and correct any deviations or gaps (1910.147(c)(6)(i)(B)).

## What the inspection includes

- **Lockout procedures:** a review between the inspector and each authorized employee of that employee's responsibilities under the procedure (1910.147(c)(6)(i)(C)).
- **Tagout procedures:** a review between the inspector and each authorized **and** affected employee of their responsibilities, including the limits of tags (1910.147(c)(6)(i)(D)).

Group meetings between the inspector and the employees who use the procedure can satisfy the review requirement.

## Certification

The employer certifies each periodic inspection (1910.147(c)(6)(ii)). The certification identifies:

- the machine or equipment the procedure was used on,
- the date of the inspection,
- the employees included,
- the person who performed the inspection.

## What happens when problems are found

If the inspection shows that someone doesn't follow the procedure correctly, or the procedure itself is wrong, the procedure must be corrected and the employees retrained (1910.147(c)(7)(iii)(B)). In Soteria Field, a failed periodic inspection can open a LOTO retraining trigger for the employees involved.

# Removal of locks and restart (29 CFR 1910.147(e))

Before locks and tags come off and energy is restored, the authorized employee must make sure the machine and the people around it are ready.

## The machine — 1910.147(e)(1)

Inspect the work area:

- remove nonessential items such as tools, parts and rags;
- make sure machine components are operationally intact, with guards reinstalled.

## The people — 1910.147(e)(2)

1. Check the work area to make sure **all employees are safely positioned or removed** (1910.147(e)(2)(i)).
2. **After** the lockout or tagout devices have been removed, and **before** the machine is started, notify the affected employees that the devices are off (1910.147(e)(2)(ii)).

## Each person removes only their own lock — 1910.147(e)(3)

Each lockout or tagout device must be removed by the employee who applied it. **Never remove someone else's lock.**

## A lock left behind

If the person who applied a lock is not available, for example they went home with the key, the device may be removed **only under the direction of the employer**. This requires specific procedures and training that are written into the site's program. Those procedures must include at least (1910.147(e)(3)(i)–(iii)):

1. verifying that the authorized employee who applied the device is **not at the facility**;
2. making **all reasonable efforts to contact** that employee to tell them their device has been removed;
3. making sure the employee knows this **before they resume work** at the facility.

In practice this means a supervisor follows the written removal procedure. A coworker never just cuts the lock.

## Restart

Once the area is clear, guards are back on, everyone is accounted for and the affected employees are notified, the machine can be restarted using the normal start procedure.

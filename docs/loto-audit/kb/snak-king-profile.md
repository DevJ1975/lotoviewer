## Snapshot

- **Company:** Snak King Corp — privately owned US snack-food manufacturer; private-label **and**
  proprietary-brand producer. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King);
  [snakking.com](https://www.snakking.com/))
- **Founded:** **1978**, originally to produce **pork rinds for a single private-label customer**.
  ([snakking.com/history](https://www.snakking.com/history/);
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King))
- **HQ / main plant:** **City of Industry ("Industry"), California** — relocated here in **1994**.
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King);
  [snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities](https://www.snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities/))
- **Scale:** one of the largest US snack manufacturers; **~950 employees**; **600,000+ sq ft** of
  combined manufacturing/warehouse space (company materials cite a total footprint **exceeding
  700,000 sq ft** across sites). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King);
  [snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities](https://www.snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities/))
- **Ownership:** Founder-led by the **Levin family** (Barry Levin, chairman/CEO; joined 1979, bought
  a one-third interest shortly after). Received a **private-equity investment from Falfurrias Capital
  Partners**. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King);
  [Business North Carolina](https://businessnc.com/charlotte-based-falfurrias-invests-in-californias-snak-king/);
  [Snack Food & Wholesale Bakery](https://www.snackandbakery.com/articles/111890-snak-king-receives-investment-from-private-equity-firm))
- **Quality/certs:** Kosher certification & inspection programs in place **25+ years**
  (OU-listed company). ([OU Kosher](https://oukosher.org/companies/snak-king/);
  [snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities](https://www.snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities/))

## History (timeline)

- **1978** — Founded by three investor groups (a pork-rind manufacturer, a distributor, and a
  manufacturing facility) to produce **pork rinds** for one private-label customer. Mike Levin was
  among the early investors. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King);
  [snakking.com/history](https://www.snakking.com/history/))
- **1979** — Barry Levin (current chairman/CEO) joins; soon buys a one-third interest.
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King))
- **1982** — Moves from an original **~1,200 sq ft** downtown Los Angeles space to a **50,000 sq ft**
  facility; **introduces the El Sabroso® brand**. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King))
- **1984** — Adds **popcorn and caramel corn**; later expands into **cheese puffs and cheese curls**.
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King))
- **1994** — Relocates to **City of Industry, CA** (cited as a 140,000 sq ft site at the time).
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King);
  [snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities](https://www.snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities/))
- **2000** — Acquires the **corn-chip, popcorn, and extruded-snack lines of Granny Goose** (West Coast
  brand). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King))
- **Dec 2011** — Acquires **C.J. Vitner Company**, a Chicago-based snack manufacturer/distributor —
  roughly **doubling Snak King's workforce and floor space**.
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King);
  [Snack Food & Wholesale Bakery](https://www.snackandbakery.com/articles/107594-snak-king-corp-acquires-c-j-vitner-co);
  [PotatoPro](https://www.potatopro.com/news/2012/snak-king-corp-acquires-cj-vitner-company))

## Plants & facilities

- **City of Industry, CA (HQ + primary manufacturing):** the main national-market plant. Company
  materials cite a **277,000 sq ft** facility producing **organic, kosher, Hispanic, and traditional
  snacks** under The Whole Earth, El Sabroso, Granny Goose, Jensen's Orchard, and Snak King brands.
  This is the **"Snak King – COI"** site in scope for this audit.
  ([snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities](https://www.snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities/);
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King))
- **Southern California:** the COI campus spans multiple buildings; the Stephens St (Industry, CA)
  address is the public plant address. ([Yelp listing](https://www.yelp.com/biz/snak-king-industry))
  **[unverified — confirm exact building/line count on site]**
- **Freeport / Chicago area, Illinois (Vitner's):** the acquired Midwest operation was reported
  **expanded to ~220,000 sq ft**, with **seven production lines, ~300 employees, and 31 packaging
  machines**; distribution from **Chicago and Elk Grove Village, IL, and Merrillville, IN**.
  ([Snack Food & Wholesale Bakery — "A royal snack plant"](https://www.snackandbakery.com/articles/86403-a-royal-snack-plant);
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King))
- **Distribution:** the company describes distribution across the US "from two strategically located
  distribution facilities in **California and Illinois**."
  ([snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities](https://www.snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities/))

## What they manufacture (product categories)

Snak King makes a broad **salty-snack** range, both **private label** and **branded**:

- **Tortilla chips** and **corn chips** (and pellet/veggie-flavored chips)
- **Potato chips** / popped crisps
- **Extruded snacks** — baked, fried, puffed, and **curled cheese puffs & cheese curls**
- **Popcorn** — including **caramel corn** and **kettle corn**
- **Pork rinds / pork cracklins** (the founding product)
- **Veggie chips** (pellet snacks)
- **Nuts, nut clusters** (tree-nut and peanut), **nut-butter snacks**, and **trail mixes**

([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snak_King);
[Grokipedia summary](https://grokipedia.com/page/snak_king);
[snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities](https://www.snakking.com/manufacturing-capabilities/);
[Snack Food & Wholesale Bakery](https://www.snackandbakery.com/articles/86402-snak-kings-new-chips-off-the-old-block))

### In-house / proprietary brands

- **El Sabroso®** — Hispanic/traditional snacks (introduced 1982).
- **Granny Goose®** — corn chips, popcorn, extruded snacks (lines acquired 2000).
- **Jensen's Orchard®** — branded snack line.
- **The Whole Earth®** — organic / Non-GMO, simple-ingredient line.

## Process lines & equipment you will see

This maps Snak King's product lines to the **typical equipment classes** an auditor will find on the
floor. Use it to anticipate which manufacturer's machines (see `manufacturers.md`) appear where, and
which **energy sources** each line implies. Line-level equipment is the standard configuration for
these product types; verify the specific machines against the placards and nameplates on site.
**[unverified at the line level — confirm against the floor and the machine nameplates on site]**

### Tortilla / corn-chip line
- **Bulk handling:** flour/corn (often nixtamalized masa) silos, conveyors, and feeders → **gravity
  + electrical** (drive motors).
- **Mixing / forming:** **dough mixers** (see Shaffer/AMF horizontal mixers) and **sheeters/cutters**
  → large drive motors, rotating/inertia hazards, sometimes **hydraulic** bowl tilt.
- **Baking & frying:** **ovens** and **continuous fryers** (Heat and Control class) → **thermal /
  hot-oil**, **gas-fired burners + gas train**, **electrical**.
- **Seasoning:** **seasoning tumblers / coating drums** (Heat and Control Spray Dynamics class) →
  **rotating** drums, electrical.
- **Product conveying:** **FastBack® horizontal-motion conveyors** (Heat and Control) → **electrical /
  VFD**, rotating-to-linear drive.

### Extruded cheese-puff / cheese-curl line
- **Extruders** (cooking extruders) → high-torque **electrical** drive, rotating screw, thermal.
- **Fryer or oven** finishing → **thermal / hot oil** or **gas**, electrical.
- **Seasoning / coating** (oil + cheese powder) → rotating drums, electrical, possibly **pneumatic**
  powder handling.

### Kettle-corn / popcorn line
- **Gas-fired kettles** → **gas (manual gas train shutoff)**, **thermal**, mechanical stirring.
- **Oil systems** (cooking oil supply/heat) → **thermal / hot oil**, electrical pumps.
- **Sifters / coolers / conveyors** → **electrical**, rotating/vibratory motion.

### Packaging (all lines)
- **Baggers / VFFS (vertical form-fill-seal)** and other fillers → **electrical / servo**,
  **pneumatic** (the signature secondary source), **thermal** (heat-seal jaws).
- **Cartoners / case packers / case sealers** (Focke, Douglas Machine class) → **electrical / servo**,
  **pneumatic**, **hot-melt glue (thermal)**, mechanical.
- **Metal detectors / checkweighers** → **electrical**.
- **Shrink wrappers / bundlers** (Douglas Machine class) → **thermal/electric tunnel heat**,
  pneumatic, electrical.
- **Palletizers** (Focke / Douglas Machine class) → **electrical / servo**, **pneumatic**, and
  **gravity** on raised lift carriages/loads.

> Industrial **mixers/blenders** (Marion Process Solutions class) and **pumps/grinders** (Marlen
> class) also appear in batter/seasoning-blend prep and protein/pork-rind handling respectively — see
> `manufacturers.md` for their energy sources and isolation points.
